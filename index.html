About

I build the kind of systems I evaluate

I'm Samira Korani, a machine learning engineer based in Galway, Ireland. I've worked in natural language processing and machine learning since 2013. Today I focus on production AI: evaluating LLM applications, building knowledge graphs and GraphRAG systems, and designing agentic workflows in Python, with the observability to see what they are doing.

My experience includes AI work in financial services and insurance, and co-founding Tripoh, a travel assistant built on LLMs and a tourism knowledge graph. My research background is in relation extraction and knowledge graph construction, and I have taught on the Knowledge Graphs (2022) and Programming I and II (2023) modules at NUI Galway, now the University of Galway.

I grew up in Iran, where a long-running question, how to live my life my way, took me through languages, cultures, music and dance before it led me to technology.
